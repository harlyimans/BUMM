About BUMM

More than just casting.

PT. Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa (BUMM) was established in 1983 and is a subsidiary of Bakrie Autoparts, based in Jatiuwung, Tangerang. We specialize in auto components and general casting — foundry manufacturing built to meet the demands of Indonesia's automotive and heavy-equipment industry.
