ABOUT US

PT Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa (BUMM), is a trusted iron casting manufacturer based in Jatiuwung, Tangerang, Indonesia.

Committed to prioritizing customer satisfaction by optimizing production processes, protecting the environment, and maintaining high standards of occupational health and safety.

Learn About BUMM

Our History

  • 1983

    PT Bina Usaha Mandiri was established to support Indonesia’s growing industrial and manufacturing needs.

  • 1993

    The company changed its name to PT Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa (BUMM).

  • 2006

    Ownership was transferred to PT Kencana Untian Prima.

  • 2011

    BUMM became part of PT Bakrie Autoparts, strengthening its position within the automotive and manufacturing industry.
