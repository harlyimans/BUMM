Our Management

Our management.

The commissioner and directors guiding PT Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa, backed by the experience of the Bakrie Autoparts group.

Company Profile Vision & Mission Corporate Value Management Facility

V. Bimo Kurniatmoko

Commissioner

V. Bimo Kurniatmoko holds a Master of Engineering in Telecommunications from the University of Melbourne, Australia, and a Bachelor of Electrical Engineering from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB).

A skilled business strategist, he brings broad expertise in project management, with a strong ability to transform strategic plans into actionable solutions and benchmark performance against key operational targets.

He concurrently serves as President Director of PT Bakrie Autoparts and PT Braja Mukti Cakra, and Director/Chief Operation Officer at PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk. Previously, he held various positions including Head of the Project Management Office at PT Bakrie Global Ventura (2016–2020), Director and CEO of PT Inovasi Teknologi Nusantara, a subsidiary of Bakrie Global (2015–2018), VP of Technology Operations at PT Bakrie Telecom Tbk (2013–2015), and Senior Account Manager at PT Ericsson Indonesia (2007–2013).

Mahzil Febri

Director

Mahzil Febri serves as a Director of PT Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa and Director of PT Bakrie Autoparts. He is also a member of the Senior Management of PT Bakrie & Brothers Tbk.
