About Us

A foundry built on four decades of casting experience.

PT. Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa (BUMM) is a domestic-investment (PMDN) foundry company and a subsidiary of PT. Bakrie Autoparts, specializing in ferrous casting and finish machining for Indonesia's automotive and heavy-equipment industries.

Company Profile Vision & Mission Corporate Value Management

BUMM specializes in the foundry industry, producing casting and finish machining (FC & FCD grey and ductile iron) to meet the needs of automotive spare-part components, diesel engine components, heavy-duty parts, water pump components, concrete railway sleepers, and other general casting work.

The company began operating under the name PT. BUM (Bina Usaha Mandiri). In 1993, a change of ownership brought the company under its current name, PT. Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa. Development continued in 1996 with a factory expansion to provide more adequate production facilities, and commercial production started in 1997 with an installed capacity target of 6,000 tons per year.

As part of the Bakrie Autoparts group, BUMM combines an established automotive-parts foundry background with the scale and resources of a larger industrial group — supplying directly into OEM supply chains for automotive and heavy-equipment manufacturers across Indonesia.
