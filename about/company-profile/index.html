About Us

DECADES OF FOUNDRY EXCELLENCE.

PT Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa (BUMM) specializes in iron casting and finish machining for automotive, heavy equipment, agriculture, railway, and general industrial applications.

Company Profile Vision & Mission Corporate Value Management Facility

PT Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa (BUMM) specializes in ferrous foundry manufacturing, producing FC (grey cast iron) and FCD (ductile cast iron) components, along with finish machining services. Its products serve five key industry segments: automotive, heavy equipment, agriculture, railway components, and general casting, including applications in automotive spare parts, diesel engines, water pumps, and concrete railway sleepers.

The company originally operated under the name PT Bina Usaha Mandiri (PT BUM). Following a change in ownership in 1993, it adopted its current name, PT Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa. In 1996, the company expanded its factory to enhance production facilities, and commercial production commenced in 1997, with a targeted installed annual production capacity of 6,000 tons.

As a subsidiary of Bakrie Autoparts, BUMM combines its established foundry expertise with the industrial capabilities of the Bakrie group. Through its commitment to manufacturing quality and continuous improvement, BUMM provides casting solutions to meet the diverse requirements of automotive, heavy equipment, agricultural machinery, railway, and general industrial applications.
