Company Profile Vision & Mission Corporate Value Facility

Vision

To become a leader of the iron cast and metal foundry industry in Indonesia, crafting a wide range of products for automotive components and general iron casting.

Mission

  • As a company engaged in the ferrous foundry, general casting & machining industry, always prioritize customer satisfaction.
  • Continuously optimize the production process to be more environmentally friendly.
  • Prioritize occupational health and safety (K3) with a constant commitment across every level of the organization.
  • Support the growth of Indonesia's automotive and heavy-equipment industry through consistent, quality-first supply.
