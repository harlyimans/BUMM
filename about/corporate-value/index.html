About Us

Built on seven operating principles.

VICTORY — Visionary, Integrity, Customer-oriented, Teamwork, Operational Excellence, Responsibility, Yield Orientation. This spirit shapes how every team at BUMM works, every day, on the foundry floor and beyond it.

Visionary — Looking ahead to anticipate what our industry and customers will need next.

Integrity — Doing what's right, consistently, even when no one's checking.

Customer-oriented — Every process is built around what makes our customers' operations succeed.

Teamwork — Complex casting work only holds together when the whole team does.

Operational Excellence — Refining the process itself, not just the output.

Responsibility — Owning the outcome — for our people, our product and the environment around the plant.

Yield Orientation — Measuring success by real, usable output — not just activity.
